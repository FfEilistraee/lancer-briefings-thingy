Elysion-9 orbits the sapphire giant Elysion, a moon coated in cobalt ice and humming ore veins. [[Director Vex]] keeps Orpheus' quotas steady from the colony's command spire, balancing the greed of the board with the safety of miners.

### Surface Conditions
- Perpetual night shrouded in aurora storms.
- Radio interference spikes whenever the ammonia squalls roll across Docktown.

### Points of Interest
- **Rustwatch-37 Hangar:** Where [[Rustwatch-37]] lancers berth and refit.
- **Transit Gate Anchorage:** A partial ring station suspended above the colony, waiting for funding to finish.
