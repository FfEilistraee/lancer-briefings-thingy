The Transit Gate Anchorage is a skeletal ring threaded with scaffolding and temporary bulkheads. Half the spokes are power-positive and the rest are still dark, waiting on the next shipment of superstructure from [[Elysion-Prime]].

### Deck Notes
- **Anchor Deck:** Docking pylons for freighters, armored by overlapping plating.
- **Spindle Control:** Houses the navigation core and [[Rustwatch-37]] flight coordinators.
- **Quarantine Wards:** Converted cargo pods that now hold inbound passengers for screenings.

### Hooks
- The unfinished spokes hide sealed contractor habs with disputed ownership.
- Gate calibrators require exotic fuel—rumors point to [[Elysion-9]]'s deeper mines.
