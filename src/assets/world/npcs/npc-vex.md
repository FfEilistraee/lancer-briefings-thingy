Director [[Vex]] is a tall man from the [[Rho-Kassandre]] system. He was assigned by the [[Orpheus Extraction & Survey]] board to keep the colony profitable without burning through crews.

### Personality
- Tough, welcoming, and kind to hard-working people.
- Intolerant of lies or corporate double-speak.

### Hooks
- Needs trusted pilots to escort ore shipments.
- Knows all the rumors about the [[Transit Gate]] network.
