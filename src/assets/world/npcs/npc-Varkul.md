### Physical Description:
[[Varkul]] is a tall [[Kovashi]] man in his mid-20s, standing around 185 cm. He wears high-status handmade [[Kovashi]] clothing known as a [[Tuurka]], adorned with bright colors, rabbit furs, and beads. His long white hair is decorated with red feathers and a chain of beads known as a [[Voraan]] that hangs from one side. His face features distinctive black eyeshadow, called [[Nyshka,]] which makes his eyes appear as narrow lines, giving him a serene yet unreadable look. His eyes are a bright pinkish-red, and when opened fully, they stand out vividly. He wears a ceremonial hat called a [[Kharal]] with feathers and fur, and his ears are adorned with large earrings. His colorful [[Tuurka]] is often covered by a black [[Sarn]], a formal drape that allows him to blend in with officials while still subtly representing his people’s traditions.

### Cultural and Behavioral Profile:

[[Varkul]] is known for his calm and thoughtful demeanor, often seen with a gentle smile that hides a sharp mind. He is highly observant, carefully weighing every word before he speaks. A habit of his is to gently touch the beads of his [[Voraan]] when deep in thought, a subtle reminder of his connection to his people and their traditions. Despite his outward serenity, [[Varkul]] is driven by a fierce determination to secure a better future for the [[Kovashi]]. His primary goal is to ensure that his tribe and the [[Kovashi]] as a whole benefit from the [[Luminite]] trade, without sacrificing their way of life or the planet they call home. He seeks a delicate balance, navigating between the demands of [[Macbeth]], the corporations, and the religious beliefs of his people. He is cautious yet hopeful, believing that by working with outsiders, he can bring prosperity to the [[Kovashi]], even if it means making difficult compromises along the way.

### History:

- Born and raised on Elysian-9 within a prominent Kovashi tribe.

- Grew up with strong ties to his tribe’s traditions and beliefs.

- Secretly learned Common from Macbeth envoys during his youth.

- Became the tribe’s official translator and mediator with Macbeth.

- Played a key role in the first negotiation involving Macbeth, the corporations, and the Kovashi.
