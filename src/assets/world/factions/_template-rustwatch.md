Rustwatch-37 is the security arm of [[Orpheus Extraction & Survey]] on [[Elysion-9]]. The company fields patched-up frames and veteran pilots who know the colony's hazards better than corporate consultants ever will.

### Mandate
- Guard the Transit Gate Anchorage during refits.
- Escort high-value ore shipments to the jump network.

### Relationships
- **Director [[Vex]]:** Sees the unit as indispensable but constantly pushes them to do more with less.
- **Local Miners:** Offer tips and bribes to get Rustwatch patrols where they're needed most.
